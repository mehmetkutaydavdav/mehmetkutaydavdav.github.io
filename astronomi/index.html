    Orbital elements

    Semi-major axis sets the orbit’s size; eccentricity tells you how stretched it is. Inclination and the two orientation angles set its tilt and direction. Mean anomaly tracks progress around the orbit from a reference time. One au is roughly Earth’s average distance from the Sun.

    01 / Appearance

    What am I looking at?

    Look for pale cloud bands and the dark Cassini Division in the rings. Rotate the view to see how a broad ring system can look almost like a thin line.

    02 / How it works

    Why are the rings so thin?

    Collisions between ring particles gradually reduce their up-and-down motion. Moons also shape the rings through gravity, creating patterns and gaps.
